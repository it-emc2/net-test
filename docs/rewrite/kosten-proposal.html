Edit description and price per position on “Kosten”

Clickable mock with sample data. The grey-bordered box is the app page; everything else is explanation.

The idea
  • Every position on the Kosten page gets a “Bearbeiten” button. Staff can change its wording and its price for this one offer.
  • A changed position is marked, shows the original value crossed out, and can be reset with one tap.
  • Totals update at once. The offer PDF, the e-mail, the signing page and the Kalkulation all use the changed values, because the change is saved with the offer.
  • This replaces most cases where staff download the Word file and edit it by hand. A hand-edited offer has no online-signing link; an offer edited here keeps it.

Try it: tap “Bearbeiten” on a position, change text or price, tap “Übernehmen”. Watch the totals and the “So steht es im Angebot” box.

Decisions for you

#QuestionMy suggestion
1Who may change prices?Everyone, with a reason. Alternative: everyone may raise a price, only admins may lower one.
2Is there a limit for lowering a price?No hard limit, but a warning when a position goes below its catalog price by more than 20 %.
3May staff add a free position (own text, own price)?Yes, as a second step after this one. It covers the last common reason for hand-editing.
4What happens to “Angebot anpassen” on the send page?Keep “Hinweis hinzufügen” and the wording rules; hide the block price adjustment for new offers (old offers keep theirs).
5Which offer types?BU, BWT, HL and BL first (priced on the server). AH is priced differently and would follow separately.
